Delete Your Data

CapitalNest primarily stores information on your device. Use the steps below to permanently remove local app data.

To permanently remove application data:

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → CapitalNest → Storage → Clear data.
  2. Or uninstall CapitalNest to remove local finance data from that device.
  3. Delete any exports you saved outside the app separately.

If you contacted support by email and want that correspondence deleted, email support@capitalnest.app with the app name and your request.

If cloud sync is added in a future release, this page will be updated with remote deletion instructions.
