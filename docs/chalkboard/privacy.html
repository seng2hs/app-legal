Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

ChalkBoard is a digital whiteboard for teachers — write, graph, import media, and present while students watch over Meet or Teams.

1. Information we store

  • Board documents, pages, drawings, imported images/PDFs, and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not require an account to use the core app experience described here.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Permissions

  • Camera / photos — only when you capture or import images onto the board.
  • Storage / files — only when you import or export a PDF.
  • Internet — ads (when enabled) and optional Play Store update checks.

3. Advertising

  • Store builds of ChalkBoard may show ads through Google AdMob.
  • AdMob/Google may collect device identifiers and usage data under Google’s policies.
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s privacy

  • ChalkBoard is intended for general users / educators as described in the store listing.
  • Do not enter sensitive personal data about children beyond what a feature clearly needs.

5. Sharing

  • Your content stays on your device unless you export, share, or use an online feature that sends data.

6. Contact

  • Questions: use Support or the contact details on the Google Play listing.
  • Data deletion: see Account / Data Deletion.

We may update this policy when the app changes. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.
