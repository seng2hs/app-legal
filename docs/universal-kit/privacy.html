Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

UniversalKit ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. UniversalKit is designed with a Privacy First approach. Your information stays under your control on your device whenever possible.

Information We Collect

Information You Enter

This information is stored on your device:

  • Tool preferences and settings
  • Content you create inside tools
  • Optional imported/exported files
  • App configuration

Permissions

  • Camera / files: Only when you open a tool that needs them.
  • Internet: Ads (if enabled), updates, and optional online tools.

Advertising

Store / free builds may display Google AdMob advertisements. Google may collect advertising ID, device information, and ad performance metrics. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Purchases

If paid upgrades are offered, payments are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not store raw payment card details.

Data Storage

Core app data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a required cloud account for basic use described here.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Content leaves your device only when you export/share it or use a feature that clearly needs internet.

Children's Privacy

UniversalKit is intended for general users as described on the store listing. Do not enter sensitive children's data beyond what a feature needs.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated over time. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.
