Account / Data Deletion

Last updated: 31 October 2026

UniversalKit primarily stores data on your device. This page explains how to remove that data and how to request deletion of any data held by us (if applicable).

1. Delete data on your device

  • Open Android Settings → Apps → UniversalKit → Storage → Clear data (wording may vary by device).
  • Or uninstall UniversalKit to remove the app and its local app data from that device.

2. If you shared content

  • Files you exported (for example PDF or shared documents) live where you saved/shared them — delete those copies separately.

3. Request deletion from the developer

  • If you believe we hold personal data about you outside the device (for example support email correspondence), contact us via the Play Store listing or Support.
  • Include the app name (UniversalKit), your contact email, and what you want deleted.
  • We will respond within a reasonable time and delete or anonymize developer-held personal data when required, except where we must retain it for legal reasons.

4. Advertising partners

  • Ad networks such as Google AdMob may process device identifiers under their own policies. See Google’s tools and privacy policy for ad-related choices.
