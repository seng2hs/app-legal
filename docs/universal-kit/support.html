How to get help with UniversalKit

UniversalKit is a collection of everyday utility tools that run on your device.

Contact

  • Use the support email published on the Google Play listing for UniversalKit.
  • Include your device model, Android version, and a short description of the issue.

Common topics

  • App not opening / crash — update from Play Store, then reinstall if needed.
  • Permissions — enable Camera / Files only if you use those features.
  • Ads — store builds may show ads; special builds may hide them.
